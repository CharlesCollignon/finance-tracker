## Priority issues

### P0 — The month-close balance field rejected the number format the app prints — FIXED THIS ROUND
Number(balance.replace(",", ".")) replaces the first comma only and strips no group separator, so the English format the app itself renders (1,234.56) became 1.234.56 and the French (1 234,56) kept its space. Both NaN. The CTA was disabled={!balanceIsUsable} with no message, so every balance over a thousand met a permanently dead button in silence on the screen the product is named for. Replaced with parseTypedAmount in @finance/core, 6 tests, plus an aria-invalid message instead of a silent disable.

### P1 — Ledger amounts carry income versus expense in hue alone
TransactionsView renders unsigned amounts with TYPE_AMOUNT_CLASS; the only channel separating arriving from leaving is success versus destructive. WCAG 1.4.1 is Level A. FulfilmentDot.tsx already states the principle for its 8px dot. Fix: render the sign, which the day header already does. Command: audit

### P1 — The Bearing is unusable-by-touch as a navigation surface
Row arrows are opacity-0 until group-hover, the caret is opacity-30 (about 2.4:1, under SC 1.4.11), and BorderGlow fires on pointermove only. FigureRow's own doc says pressing a tile must not be a coin flip; on touch it is. Command: audit

### P1 — The Semantic Amount Rule fails on 17 sites
Three are a screen's lead figure. bearing/Headline.tsx argues in a comment that the lead figure must agree with toneFor, so two rules the codebase holds are in direct conflict and one has to move. Includes toneFor itself, which keeps red on a negative free figure — colouring by sign, which the rule forbids in terms.

### P2 — Server strings reach users raw
