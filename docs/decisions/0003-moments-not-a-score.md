# 0003. Moments, not a score; habit from use alone

- **Date:** 2026-10-01, confirmed 2026-10-08
- **Status:** Accepted
- **See also:** `docs/plans/QUALITY_SYNC_ENGAGEMENT_PLAN.md`, `docs/plans/EVERYDAY_PLAN.md`

## Context

The owner wanted people to come back more ("a bit addicted"). Options were
celebrating on the Plan only, full gamification (points, levels, badges), or
user-set goals and challenges.

## Decision

- **Celebrate real events where they happen:** a month closed, a run
  extended, the inbox emptied, a milestone reached. Animation, haptics and a
  push. No score of any kind.
- **No goals, budgets or challenges set by the user** (offered on
  2026-10-08 and declined). Habit comes from the app being useful every day.
- **A weekly recap**, on by default: a Monday push and a card, facts only.
- **Four warnings:** overdraft risk before the next income, the close
  reminder on reading day, a big charge tomorrow, and spending above normal
  (a « constat » inside the recap, not its own push).

## Consequences

- The landing's promise « Aucun conseil. Aucun score. » stays true.
- Budgets and goals from the early schema (`012`, `038`) are retired.

## Don't

- Add XP, levels, badges as score, streak scores, goals, budgets or
  challenges.
