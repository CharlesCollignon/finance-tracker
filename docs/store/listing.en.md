## Description (4,000 at most, both stores)

Pluclair tells you what you can still spend until your next income, with the
charges to come already counted. No ads, nothing sold, no advice: your
figures, right.

WHAT YOU HAVE LEFT
One figure as the app opens: "You have €412 until the 28th, so €21 a day".
Your recurring items — rent, subscriptions, salary — are already in it. "Can
I afford it?" tells you what a spend would change.

NOTE IT IN SECONDS
The "+" opens the add sheet: the amount, the shop, and Pluclair finds the
category. On Android, the home-screen widget shows the figure and opens the
add sheet in one tap; on both phones, so does a long press on the icon.

THE MONTH, CLOSED
Each month you read your bank's balance. Pluclair compares it with what was
noted and measures what slipped through — cash, a forgotten card. Your
figures hold up against your statement.

WHAT COMES BACK EVERY MONTH
Charges, subscriptions, income: set once, they fill each month on their own.
Pluclair spots your subscriptions and tells you when one goes up.

YOUR SAVINGS AND INVESTMENTS
PEA, securities account, life insurance, PER: your wallets, their fees and
what your funds really hold. Your net worth, property included.

FOR TWO
A shared space for the joint account: each of you sees what is shared, and
never the other's own money.

AND ALSO

- "Your year": your year in figures, to keep or share.
- Income tax return: the amounts for your boxes (donations, home help,
  childcare, PER, rents), to check on impots.gouv.fr.
- CSV and OFX statement import.
- Search across all your months.
- The Monday recap and a warning before an overdraft.
- Questions and written reads by the AI of your choice (Mistral, ChatGPT or
  Claude), on your own OpenRouter account, paid to OpenRouter.

YOUR DATA
Hosted in the European Union. No ads, no tracking from app to app, nothing
sold. Blur your amounts in one tap, lock the app with Face ID or your
fingerprint. Deleting your account, from the app, takes everything with it.

Pluclair is free, and the same on pluclair.com.
